# A-Tribute-to-Eva-Burns-Lundgren-and-Tom-Burns
This article "Mentalization and Analytical Phenomenological Psychology: Toward an Integrated Phenomenological Model of Self-Understanding and Human Consciousness" is dedicated to Eva Burns-Lundgren and Tom Burns, two distinguished London psychoanalysts and founders of Cognitive Analytic Psychotherapy. As a young psychotherapy trainee at the Ottonello Hospital in Bologna, I had the privilege of attending a lecture on mentalization delivered by Eva Burn-Lundgren. I still remember the profound intellectual and emotional impact of that experience. At the time, I was simply a trainee sitting among my professors, fascinated by ideas that would remain with me throughout my professional life. Many years later, as the founder of Analytical Phenomenological Psychology (APP), I am honored to engage in a theoretical dialogue with a tradition that inspired me as a student. This work is offered with gratitude, admiration, and deep respect.

ACKNOWLEDGEMENT

To Eva Burns-Lundgren and Tom Burns, two distinguished London psychoanalysts and founders of Cognitive Analytic Psychotherapy. As a young psychotherapy trainee, I attended a conference on mentalization by Eva Burn-Lundgren at the Ottonello Hospital in Bologna. I was deeply moved and inspired by that experience. At the time, I could not have imagined that one day I would develop my own theoretical framework, Analytical Phenomenological Psychology (APP), and engage in a dialogue with ideas that had fascinated me as a student. This work is dedicated to them with gratitude, admiration, and profound respect.
